Your letters become the path

Every key press lifts a rune stone out of the clouds into the gap. You see your spelling take shape as the way forward.

A correct spell locks the bridge

The stones turn gold and snap into a rope-bridge curve. Your hero walks across, the camera follows, and the next lantern lights.

A miss: the wisp helps you across

Your stones crumble softly into the clouds. The wisp lays the right word as pale blue stones and you still cross. Its lantern glows blue, which marks a word that comes back in review.

Typing never waits

You can start the next word while the hero is still walking. Animation never blocks the spellbook.
